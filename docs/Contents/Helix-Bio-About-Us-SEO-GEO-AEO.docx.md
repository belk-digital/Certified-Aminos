# **1\. Website Overview**

Helix Bio (helix-bio.vercel.app) is a U.S.-facing supplier of synthetic research peptides sold strictly for laboratory and research-use-only (RUO) applications. The About Us page is the site's primary trust surface: it's where a researcher or lab buyer who has already found the catalogue goes to answer one question before they order — "can I actually rely on this supplier?"

The page currently leans on broad, low-specificity language (mission/value statements without concrete detail). The rewrite keeps every section and its position exactly as designed, but replaces generic claims with verifiable, specific language around testing, classification, and documentation — the things the keyword research shows researchers are actually searching for.

Per the keyword research scope, this page intentionally avoids commercial or transactional framing (pricing, "buy," "best supplier"). It stays informational and compliance-forward, and routes conversion-minded visitors to the catalogue only through the existing CTA band at the bottom of the page.

## **Frequently Asked Questions (on-page)**

# **12\. FAQ Section**

The four Q\&As above are what's live on the page and what the FAQPage schema in Section 13 reflects. The four below are additional, schema-ready candidates for a future FAQ expansion — held separate so the visible page and the structured data stay in sync (see the AEO note in Section 16 on why that match matters).

### **What does "research use only" mean?**

Research use only (RUO) means a compound is intended solely for laboratory and scientific investigation, not for human or animal consumption, diagnosis, or treatment. RUO labeling signals that a product hasn't gone through the review process required of an approved drug.

### **What is a certificate of analysis (COA)?**

A certificate of analysis is a document reporting the results of analytical testing performed on a specific batch, typically including purity percentage, molecular weight confirmation, and the test methods used, such as HPLC or mass spectrometry.

### **How should research peptides be stored?**

Lyophilized peptides are generally more stable than reconstituted solutions and should be kept sealed, protected from light, and stored at the temperature specified on their documentation until they're needed for research use.

### **What's the difference between research-grade and pharmaceutical-grade peptides?**

Research-grade peptides are manufactured for laboratory investigation and verified against purity and identity specifications, while pharmaceutical-grade peptides go through additional regulatory review, clinical validation, and manufacturing controls required for human drug approval.
