# **1\. Website Overview**

Helix Bio is a USA-targeted supplier of synthetic research peptides sold strictly for laboratory and scientific research (Research Use Only). The /contact-us page is the site's primary support and trust-verification surface: it gives researchers a fast way to reach the team for order status, bulk/wholesale quotes, and lab-specification questions, while routing self-serve questions toward the FAQ page instead.

As with the prior Home, About Us, Shop, and FAQ deliverables, copy here is framed around the legitimate research-chemical audience — laboratory scientists, biotech researchers, and institutional buyers — rather than personal-use language.

# **2\. Search Intent Analysis**

**Primary intent:** Navigational/Local — “Helix Bio contact,” “contact peptide supplier,” “peptide company phone number.” Visitors on this specific page already know the brand and want a fast, correct way to reach a real person.

**Secondary intent:** Informational — support hours, which email to use for which purpose, and shipping/refund policy questions that the FAQ section absorbs before a visitor ever needs to submit the form.

Note: the supplied keyword research frames the site's overall priority as Commercial, since most of its terms target the shop and product pages. The /contact-us page itself is the exception — its own keyword cluster (“contact peptide supplier,” “peptide vendor customer support,” “Helix Bio contact information”) is Navigational/Local first, which is why this document treats it that way rather than importing the site-wide commercial framing wholesale.

# **14\. SEO Checklist**

* Primary keyword ("contact Helix Bio" / "contact peptide supplier") appears in the H1, meta title, meta description, and hero copy.

* Meta title is 53 characters; meta description is 143 characters — both within best-practice limits.

* Heading hierarchy has one H1, matches the sections visible in the screenshot (contact cards, form, FAQ, closing CTA), and introduces no invented headings.

* NAP (name, address, phone) details are kept consistent with “99Purity Peptides” as the legal/operating entity, matching the footer, newsletter block, and email domain already established across the site — not swapped to “Helix Bio” where that would break local-SEO/Knowledge Panel matching.

* The visible address fragment (“…Carolina 29418”) is not expanded into a fabricated full street address; it's flagged for client confirmation instead.

* FAQ content is written for FAQPage schema eligibility — clear question text, concise answer text, no marketing filler inside the answer.

* No fabricated certifications, statistics, or outcome guarantees were introduced; all figures either match the source screenshot/keyword research or are marked as placeholders pending client confirmation.

# **15\. GEO Checklist**

* The Organization \+ ContactPoint schema (Section 13\) gives AI engines and Google's Knowledge Graph a structured, citable answer for “Helix Bio contact” and “99Purity Peptides phone number” queries, rather than relying on prose alone.

* Support-hours, phone, and email are stated as plain factual sentences (not buried in a paragraph), matching the direct-answer format AI Overviews prefer for navigational/local queries.

* The Support Email vs. Orders Email distinction is kept explicit, so an AI engine can correctly route a summarized answer (“for order tracking, use X; for lab inquiries, use Y”) instead of collapsing both into one generic contact fact.

* Consistent entity naming (“99Purity Peptides” for the legal/NAP entity, “Helix Bio” for the product brand) is maintained throughout so language models don't garble the two into a single inconsistent name.

# **16\. AEO Checklist**

* FAQ section is structured as clean question-then-answer pairs so it maps directly to FAQPage schema and voice/answer-engine extraction.

* Each answer leads with the direct fact (address/ZIP, phone, hours, states served) before any supporting detail, matching how answer engines extract navigational/local facts.

* Support-hours answer states the exact days and time window in the first sentence, a strong candidate for a direct AI Overview answer card.

* The three contact-method cards (Support Email, Orders Email, Support Phone) are short and parallel in structure, formatting well as a list snippet for “how do I contact Helix Bio” style queries.
