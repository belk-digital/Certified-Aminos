### **2\. What receptors does VIP interact with?**

VIP primarily interacts with two receptor subtypes known as **VPAC1 and VPAC2**. Both belong to the G protein-coupled receptor family and have been studied extensively in molecular and cellular research.

### **3\. What is VIP used for in research?**

VIP is used as a research ligand in studies involving VPAC1/VPAC2 receptor signaling, GPCR pharmacology, neuropeptide biology, gastrointestinal signaling, immune-cell research, and peptide-receptor interactions.
